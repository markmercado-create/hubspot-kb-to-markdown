# How to create a new GP instance?

The process to create a new GP Instance is the same whether Straker is managing your translations or you are managing the entire process yourself, using docs in **[Globalization Central](https://w3.ibm.com/globalization/)** to train your team, and **[#g11n-pipeline](https://ibm-cloudplatform.slack.com/archives/C0EP2MFR7)** to ask questions.

Request your new GP Instance by writing a post to **Jordi Serratosa** in Slack channel **[#g11n-pipeline](https://ibm-cloudplatform.slack.com/archives/C0EP2MFR7)** and his team can set up a GP instance for you. You will need your `UTL20` and `PID` of your product. These can be found in **FedCat** if you don't know them. Please fill [this **excel template**](https://20462040.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/20462040/knowledge-base-files/GP%20instance%20creation.xlsx) and then send it to Jordi as an attachment to your post.
