# How long will migration/onboarding to GP take?

The migration/onboarding to Globalization Pipeline (GP) generally takes 4 weeks, but it can be 3-10 weeks depending on your specific product. Longer timelines result from projects that need several custom file filters. Product teams should plan on dedicating a resource for DevOps, development (for optional filters) and a tester.
