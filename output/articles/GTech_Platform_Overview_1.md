# GTech Platform Overview

GTech stands for **Globalization Technology**, and it encompasses a range of services primarily designed to support machine translation and human translation of documents and Program Integrated Information (PII). Additionally, GTech is equipped with translation memory capabilities, allowing for more efficient and consistent translation by reusing previously translated content. The platform serves as a comprehensive solution for global content management, enabling seamless localization across various languages and formats. By integrating advanced translation technologies and leveraging translation memory, GTech optimizes the translation process, ensuring faster delivery and higher quality across multilingual projects.
