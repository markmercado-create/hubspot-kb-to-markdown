# Billing FAQ

## Common Billing Questions

### When am I charged?

You are charged at the start of each billing cycle.

### Can I get a refund?

Yes, refunds are available within **30 days** of purchase. Contact [billing@example.com](mailto:billing@example.com).

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal.
