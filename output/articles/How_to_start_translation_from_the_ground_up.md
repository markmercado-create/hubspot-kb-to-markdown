# How to start translation from the ground up?

### Get you started on Globalization Pipeline (GP) to translate your software UI content:

1. There is a super cool site that will really help you get a vibe for the process -- [Globalization Central](https://w3.ibm.com/globalization/). Spending 30 minutes there watching a couple of quick videos and reading some overview stuff will be really helpful.
2. Join the IBM private Slack channel to be informed of all Globalization Community happenings. There are regular meetings and announcements.
3. Decide whether you have the time and staff to self-serve on the translation process or you would like help from our vendor Straker Translations ([options and costs info](https://ibm.box.com/s/abf3u6adch4hdlcrtx8mp7vmjm7brvm2)).
4. If you want Straker help, contact IBMers who can introduce you and help coordinate. [@Nathalie Caron](https://ibm-cloudplatform.slack.com/team/W4F20RAGZ) and [@enric\_arau](https://ibm-cloudplatform.slack.com/team/W9RATFQHW) are great help to coordinate.
5. If you want to self-serve then join Slack channel [#g11n-pipeline](https://ibm-cloudplatform.slack.com/archives/C0EP2MFR7) and ask for a GP instance to be created for you. You can then follow process in Globalization Central to on board yourself.

- Time to on board to the GP tools? Maybe a week or two.
- Time to run a translation cycle? Once on boarded each translation request (TR) is only a few days, depending on word-count of how big your job is. For an ongoing sprint (or month) cycle it would be 3 - 5 day-ish.  But on your FIRST translation you will have a huge volume and it will take a couple weeks.

Is your product part of Cloud Products published on [cloud.ibm.com/docs](http://cloud.ibm.com/docs) ? On-prem type product published on [IBM.co/docs](http://ibm.co/docs)? If it's on [cloud.ibm.com/docs](http://cloud.ibm.com/docs), then speak with Carolyn Carpenter about on boarding to her docs process.
