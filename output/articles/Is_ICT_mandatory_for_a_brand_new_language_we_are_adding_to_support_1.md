# Is ICT mandatory for a brand new language we are adding to support?

ICT can be necessary if you did not provide screenshots during the initial translation (RTR), but it is not mandatory. Without screenshots, translation quality might be affected due to the lack of context. However, ICT is not mandatory, we only recommend performing ICT.
