# ICT Policy

- ICT is no longer mandatory on each GA release.
- ICT is performed as needed based on ROI and the magnitude of product changes and the potential for significant impact on customer experience or IBM reputation.
- PII 'Translation with context' (screenshots) substantially improves quality, eliminating the need for frequent ICTs.
