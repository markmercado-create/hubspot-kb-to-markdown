# GB18030 Certification Training & Execution Flow

**Audience**: IBM Product Teams, Dev Leads, Globalization Stakeholders   
**Trainer**: Straker Team (Chinese Dev) & Globalization Experts

#### **Phase 1: Kickoff & Planning**

- **Activity:**
  1. **Kickoff Call**
     1. 1. **Owner:** Straker + IBM
        2. **Description:** Set context, define objectives, identify attendees.
  2. **Training Plan Review**
     1. 1. **Owner:** Straker
        2. **Description:** Present training manual, agenda, roles, and expectations.
  3. **Identify Products**
     1. 1. **Owner:** IBM
        2. **Description:** IBM to confirm the software requiring certification.

#### Phase 2: Training Delivery

- **Module**:
  1. **Module 1: Overview of GB18030**
     1. 1. **Content:** History, regulation, legal implications
        2. **Format:** Live + Slide Deck
  2. **Module 2: Technical Deep-dive**
     1. 1. **Content:** Encoding, Unicode, CCSIDs, code pages, UTF-8/32
        2. **Format:** Live with demo
  3. **Module 3: Certification**
     1. 1. **Content:** Documentation, test samples, validation process
        2. **Format:** Live + Q&A
  4. **Module 4: Common Pitfalls**
     1. 1. **Content:** UI rendering issues, political sensitivity, unsupported characters
        2. **Format:**Case Studies
  5. **Module 5: IBM Specific Submission Needs**
     1. 1. **Content:** Manuals, UI screenshots, encoding specs
        2. **Format:**Customer training

📎 Materials: Certification Manual, Slide Deck, Sample Reports, FAQ, Appendix Tables

🎓 Format: 1–2 sessions, 1.5 hrs each, plus follow-up Q&A session

#### 

#### Phase 3: Certification Execution Flow

> ```
> Step-by-Step Execution (Post-Training)
> ```

**Steps**:

1. **Pre-check & Scope Confirmation:**  
   1. 1. **Description:**  Confirm product readiness, scope, encoding type
      2. **Owner:** IBM + Straker
2. **Document Preparation**
   1. 1. **Description:**Product manuals, tech specs, UI samples
      2. **Owner:** IBM
3. **Review & Translation**
   1. 1. **Description:**Translate manuals/UI into Simplified Chinese
      2. **Owner:** Straker
4. **Lab Submission Prep**
   1. 1. **Description:**Package files, format screenshots, align to template
      2. **Owner:** Straker Team (Chinese Dev)
5. **Lab Testing (China)**
   1. 1. **Description:**  Conduct tests (rendering, input/output, encoding)
      2. **Owner:**Government-certified lab
6. **Issue Resolution**
   1. 1. **Description:** Fix failures in rendering/input/output
      2. **Owner:**IBM (with support from Straker)
7. **Validation Report**
   1. 1. **Description:** Collect lab results + screenshots
      2. **Owner:** Lab + Straker
8. **Approval & Certification Issuance**
   1. 1. **Description:** Receive certificate, update compliance record
      2. **Owner:** Lab + IBM
9. **Final Report to IBM**
   1. 1. **Description:** Full certification archive with results
      2. **Owner:** Straker

#### **🧭 Roles & Responsibilities**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | **Responsibility** |
| **IBM Dev Team** | Provide product details, encoding info, manuals |
