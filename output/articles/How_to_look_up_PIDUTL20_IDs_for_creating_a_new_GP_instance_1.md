# How to look up PID/UTL20 IDs for creating a new GP instance?

In order to get the PID and UTL20, you should ask your Product Manager. Here is a link that might be of help: <https://w3.ibm.com/globalization/develop/globalizationpipeline/#prerequisites> . Sometimes you probably want to use that UTL20 if there is another product in the product-family that is translated. Plus, you can ask the DevOps person of your product what Memory Domains they use at the same time.
