# How will we know the Straker effort billed if we do not estimate the # of hours ahead of ICT?

ICT effort is based on the number of screenshots that a tester has to review.  Straker will charge the effort in  minutes/hours for the work perform. Product development team no longer needs to estimate the ICT effort ahead of the ICT submission, the globalization pipeline process will indicate to Straker the number of screenshot in scope for ICT.
