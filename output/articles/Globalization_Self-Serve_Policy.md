# Globalization Self-Serve Policy

Product development teams are responsible for

- the submission of their PII translation and ICT requests via Globalization Pipeline
- capturing and attaching English screenshots on the translation requests for context and on the ICT requests for PII testing (English and translated screenshots)
- perform FVT and GVT as part of their development process
- reach out to GTech team (slack #globalization-at-IBM or #g11n-pipeline) to request assistance from the global vendor Straker for the above tasks
- consult the [globalization champions’ meetings](https://w3.ibm.com/globalization/champions/champion_meeting) recap of the globalization’s multi-year transformation and closeout session to find the IBM teams now responsible for accessibility, penetration testing, and GB18030 compliance.
