# What is the expected turnaround time for an ICT?

The expected turnaround time for ICT via Globalization Pipeline is based on the following metrics :

- Minimum/default 5 business days - 1-50 screenshots to review
- Every 50 additional screenshots = add one more business day

  - ex. 51-100 screens = 6 days
  - 101-150 = 7 days
  - 151-200 = 8 days
  - 201-250 = 9 days
  - 251-300 = 10 days
  - 301-350 = 11 days
- The ability to apply priority parameter is available when requesting ICT so Straker has the ability to prioritize to the best of their ability.
