# Once issues reported during ICT are fixed, can the Straker tester review the fixed issues?

Once the defect reported is fixed, you need to request a new ICT for Straker if you wish for a tester to review again, this is not mandatory and is entirely the product development team decision. The expected results will be 0 defects (meaning issue solved)
