# Translation Costs

### Translation Costs

Please note that there is a charge for both MT and human translation through IBM Globalization Pipeline. Please contact the Globalization Pipeline team for charge details.

### How Often should a Development Team Submit Requests for Human Translation?

While Globalization Pipeline enables development teams to submit human translation requests as often as they like, if the strings you are submitting for translation are not stable (i.e., they may be changed or deleted as the software UI work is completed), **you may incur unnecessary translation costs**.

Finding the correct cadence for submitting human translation requests is up to the development and offering team to determine. We can gladly advise you on what makes sense for your particular situation. See [When To Request Human Translation.](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/mt-and-human-translation)
