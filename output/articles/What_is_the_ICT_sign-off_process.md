# What is the ICT sign-off process?

ICT is closed when all testers finish screenshots review and return corrected translations and defect report. There is no formal sign-off (back and forth until all defects fixed and approved) .  The intent is that if the product development team fixes defects and need them approved, another ICT needs to be requested.
