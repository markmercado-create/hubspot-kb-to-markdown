# What is the translation cost of MT api?

The translation cost is $40 USD/1-million-char. No DOU needed it's just billed to your department. BTW, $40 is about 500 HTML pages or about 200,000 English words. The service is very cost effective. However, the MT inside of GP is no cost. The charges will be billed back to the Division/Cost Center of the submitting user.
