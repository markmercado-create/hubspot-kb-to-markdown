# Process

Globalization Pipeline (GP) is your self-serve platform designed for integrating software translation in the DevOps pipeline. The information on these pages assumes that you are familiar with GP; however if you are new to GP and wish to use the cloud native service to request [translation](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/translation-with-context) and [testing](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/in-context-testing-ict) of your Product UI, you first need to follow the on-boarding steps.

Click on these links to get your team up and running!

[What is Globalization Pipeline](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/globalization-pipeline-introduction)

[On-boarding steps](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/onboarding)

[Integrating GP into DevOps](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/integrating-gp-into-devops)
