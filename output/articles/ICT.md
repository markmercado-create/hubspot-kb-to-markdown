# ICT

![](https://20462040.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/20462040/image-png-Jul-22-2024-07-06-28-7471-AM.png)

Globalization Pipeline offers services allowing the product development teams to work from one platform and execute all tasks related to PII translation and testing into one integrated self-serve process for quick and simple continuous delivery.

[Translation with context](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/translation-with-context) allows product development teams to attach references (English screenshots, links or documents) to the regular Globalization Pipeline Translation Request (TR).  This capability significantly improves the quality of the PII translation, over time will allow to reduce the frequency of ICT before a GA saving significant translation costs.  At some point, for very small UI updates - low risk, small scope - ICT may not even be necessary.

[In-context testing (ICT)](https://kb.strakertranslations.com/en/knowledge/in-context-testing-ict) is a self-serve workflow allowing the product development teams to upload English and translated UI screenshots, run and ICT readiness check, and submit a request to the Global vendor Straker to perform ICT.  During ICT, Straker will review and compare English and translated screenshots in the Straker workbench, and will have the ability to report defects.
